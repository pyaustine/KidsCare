Privacy & Data Use Policy

Last Updated: [23 November 2023]

KidsCare is dedicated to ensuring the privacy and security of your information when you use KidsCare AI (hereinafter referred to as "the App").

1. Information We Collect

KidsCare AI may collect personal information, such as name, age, and responses to screening questions. This information is solely used for generating screening results and is not associated with user accounts.

Non-personal information, like device details and IP addresses, is collected for analytical purposes to enhance KidsCare AI's performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Personal information is used exclusively for providing accurate screening results and is not shared with third parties. Non-personal information is utilized to improve KidsCare AI's functionality and overall user experience.

3. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures, including encryption, to protect your data from unauthorized access and maintain confidentiality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties unless required by law or with your explicit consent. Aggregated and anonymized data may be used for research or statistical purposes, ensuring individual privacy is maintained.

5. Changes to the Policy

KidsCare reserves the right to update and modify this KidsCare AI Privacy & Data Use Policy. Users will be notified of any significant changes.

6. Contact Information

For questions or concerns regarding your privacy or this policy, please contact support@kidscare.ai

By using KidsCare AI, you acknowledge your understanding and agreement to the terms outlined in this Privacy & Data Use Policy.
